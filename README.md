# Stack measurement

1. Mount the phone square to the measurement plane. Lay the whole stack flat on a plain black background and leave background visible around all sides. The stack can be rotated within the photo.
2. Tap **Take photo** to capture a full-size image with the phone camera or **Choose photo** to open an existing one. Match the camera, zoom, image resolution, lighting, and distance to the measured face used during calibration.
3. Check that the cyan rectangle fits the stack body and excludes protruding studs. The app converts each dimension from original image pixels using `mm = 0.157556 × px − 0.657387`.
4. **Copy mm pair** copies the length and width in millimetres, in that order. **Copy pixel pair** copies the original pixel measurements in the same order.

If the table or stand appears around the black platform, keep the colored stack close to the center of the photo. The app can look for strong colors in the center when the rest of the frame is cluttered. Use a plain contrasting background for white or gray bricks.
